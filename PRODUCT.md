# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Health-conscious individuals in India managing chronic conditions (Diabetes, PCOS, Anaemia, Obesity), GLP-1 users needing protein-focused nausea-safe meal plans, dietitians as a clinical support tool, and researchers working on Indian nutrition data. Users access the app on mobile and desktop, often during meal prep or clinical consultation.

## Product Purpose

AaharAI NutriSync provides evidence-based Indian nutrition guidance grounded in ICMR-NIN 2024 standards and the IFCT 2017 food composition database. It solves the gap where generic AI health tools give Westernized or inaccurate nutrition advice for Indian users. Success looks like users consistently meeting their RDA targets through personalized meal plans and food tracking.

## Brand Personality

Modern + Professional + Approachable. Voice is clinically authoritative but warm — like a knowledgeable dietitian who speaks your language. Indian-first in food examples, portion sizes, and cultural context. Not sterile/medical, not casual/playful.

## Anti-references

- Typical Western health apps (MyFitnessPal, Noom) — this is Indian-first
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics — avoid cookie-cutter admin/KPI layouts
- Overly sterile clinical/medical app look — keep warmth

## Design Principles

1. **Indian-first, always** — Every food, portion, and recommendation must be grounded in Indian dietary context. Never default to Western equivalents.
2. **Clinical authority with warmth** — Evidence-backed (ICMR-NIN, IFCT) but delivered in an approachable, encouraging tone. Like a friendly dietitian, not a textbook.
3. **Progressive complexity** — Simple at first glance, powerful when needed. Dashboard should make immediate sense to a new user while offering depth for power users.
4. **Trust through transparency** — Show sources for every claim. Users should see "according to IFCT 2017..." not just a number. API key handling must be transparently secure.
5. **Responsive by necessity** — Indian users access health tools on diverse devices. The UI must work flawlessly on both ₹10k Android phones and high-end laptops.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Body text must achieve 4.5:1 contrast minimum. The interface should accommodate reduced motion preferences. Multi-language support (English + 5 Indian languages) is a core feature, not an afterthought. Color choices must remain distinguishable for users with color vision deficiency.
